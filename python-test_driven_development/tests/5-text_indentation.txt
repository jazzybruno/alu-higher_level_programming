The ``5-text_indentation`` module
=================================

    >>> text_indentation = __import__('5-text_indentation').text_indentation

Each delimiter is followed by two new lines, and lines are stripped:

    >>> text_indentation("Hello. How are you? Fine: thanks")
    Hello.
    <BLANKLINE>
    How are you?
    <BLANKLINE>
    Fine:
    <BLANKLINE>
    thanks

Leading and trailing spaces are removed:

    >>> text_indentation("   Holberton.   School   ")
    Holberton.
    <BLANKLINE>
    School

Text without delimiters:

    >>> text_indentation("No delimiters here")
    No delimiters here

An empty string prints nothing:

    >>> text_indentation("")
